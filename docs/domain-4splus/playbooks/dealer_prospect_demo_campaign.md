# Playbook: Dealer Prospect Demo Campaign

Audience: prospecting (Vo Technology selling 4S PLUS). Source: CRM lead scoring.

Steps:
1. Python scores every lead with reasons (fit, volume, pain, signals, recency).
2. Hot leads (65 plus): a follow up task is prepared in TypeScript, approval
   gated, offering a live demo on the prospect's own data.
3. Warm leads (40 to 64): receive the One Car Journey story, follow up in 3 days.
4. Cold leads: monthly education sequence only, no direct pitch.
5. Content calendar slots for prospecting always carry one CTA: book a live demo.

Proof material allowed: the One Car Journey, JobCard lifecycle discipline
(W.I.P to Ready for invoicing), Actual versus Sold hours, Create Requests to
Issues parts flow, survey after delivery.

KPI: demos booked per 100 hot and warm leads, show rate, tier accuracy reviewed
monthly with the sales team. Guardrail: no guaranteed results claims, no
unverified percentages, no competitor claims without a source.
