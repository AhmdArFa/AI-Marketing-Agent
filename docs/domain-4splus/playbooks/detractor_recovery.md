# Playbook: Survey Detractor Recovery and Promoter Referral

Audience: dealer marketing. Source: Survey pillar, score after delivery.

Detractors (score 6 or less):
1. Segment is human call only. The agent never sends an automated offer first.
2. The agent prepares a call brief: JobCard history, operations done, survey
   score, and the likely friction points from the card (waiting time, cost
   clarity, parts delay).
3. A human calls, listens, and logs the outcome in Follow Customer.
4. Only after a resolved outcome may an offer be prepared, approval gated.

Promoters (score 9 or 10):
1. WhatsApp thank you with a review link and a referral ask, approval gated.
2. Their words, with permission, become proof content for prospecting.

KPI: detractors contacted within 24 hours, recovery rate, reviews collected.
Guardrail: never argue with a score, never incentivize changing a score.
